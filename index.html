How it works

Four steps from shoebox to spreadsheet.

01

Capture

Send receipts via phone photo, forwarded email, or SMS. No app to install for capture — use the channels you already use.

02

Extract

AI reads each receipt and pulls vendor, date, total, tax, and itemized line details. Edge cases get queued for quick review.

03

Match

Bank and credit card transactions, pulled securely through Plaid, are matched to the right receipt automatically.

04

Export

Clean, categorized records export to QuickBooks, Xero, or a generic CSV. Month-end becomes a five-minute task.

Data handling

Boring, on purpose.

We pull only the financial data needed to do the job, store it carefully, and never sell or share it. The user's accounting workflow is the only thing it's used for. Full detail lives in our Privacy Policy.

  • Plaid Products Transactions and Balance. No identity, auth, investments, or liabilities data.
  • Purpose Matching user-submitted receipts to bank transactions and generating accounting exports.
  • Storage Encrypted at rest, scoped per authenticated user, deleted on account closure.
  • Sharing No third-party sale or sharing. Exports go only to destinations the user authorizes.
  • Access Single-tenant access controls. Plaid tokens stored in encrypted secret storage.
About

Built by operators, for operators.

Ledgerly began as an internal tool — a way to stop losing receipts, stop manually reconciling them against bank statements, and stop spending hours every month on bookkeeping that should be automatic.

It is built and maintained by a small team of small-business operators who wanted the product for their own books before offering it to anyone else.

The current closed beta runs against real operating businesses — real receipt formats, real bank feeds, real month-end deadlines — to harden the product before any wider release.

Expansion to other small business owners and sole proprietors will follow once the closed beta proves reliable at the messiness of real operations.
