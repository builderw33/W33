Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 May 2026  ·  Last updated: 19 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how W33 ("W33," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use our automated bookkeeping service (the "Service"). It applies to all users of the Service.

"W33" is a working title for a product currently in closed beta. By using the Service you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information — name, email address, and authentication credentials used to create and secure your account.
  • Receipts and documents — receipt images, PDFs, emails, and SMS messages you submit, along with the data extracted from them (vendor, date, amounts, tax, and line items).
  • Communications — messages you send to us for support or other inquiries.

Financial account information (via Plaid)

When you choose to connect a bank or credit card account, we use Plaid Inc. ("Plaid") to retrieve financial data on your behalf. Through Plaid we access:

  • Transactions — transaction history for the accounts you link, including amounts, dates, descriptions, and merchant information.
  • Balance — current and available balances for the accounts you link.

We do not request or access Plaid's Identity, Auth, Investments, Liabilities, or other products beyond Transactions and Balance.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data — log data, IP address, browser or device type, and timestamps, used to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect only to provide and improve the Service, specifically to:

  • Match receipts you submit to the correct bank or credit card transactions.
  • Categorize transactions and generate accounting exports to platforms you authorize (such as QuickBooks, Xero, Wave, or CSV).
  • Authenticate you, secure your account, and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability of the Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your data.

3. Plaid and connected financial accounts

We use Plaid to connect your financial accounts to the Service. When you link an account, you provide your credentials directly to Plaid through Plaid's secure interface — W33 does not see or store your bank login credentials.

Plaid's handling of your information is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review it. You can learn more about Plaid at plaid.com.

Access tokens issued by Plaid that allow the Service to retrieve your transaction and balance data are stored in encrypted secret storage and are used only to deliver the features described in this policy. You may disconnect a linked account at any time, which revokes our ongoing access to that account.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal or financial information. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • At your direction — when you export records to an accounting platform or destination you have authorized.
  • Service providers — with vendors who process data on our behalf (such as Plaid for account connectivity, and infrastructure and hosting providers), under contracts that require them to protect the data and use it only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety — when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of users or the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to the protections described in this policy.

5. Data storage and security

  • Financial and account data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Data is scoped per authenticated user; each user can access only their own data.
  • Plaid access tokens and other secrets are held in encrypted secret storage with restricted access.
  • Access to production systems is limited to authorized personnel and protected by access controls.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take commercially reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you close your account or request deletion, we delete your personal and financial data, except where retention is required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. You may also disconnect any linked financial account at any time.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Disconnect linked financial accounts and revoke our access.
  • Withdraw consent or object to certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
